# P1.15 Gate Decision — Multi-Horizon Baselines

Status: **not justified under the frozen promotion gates**
Date: 2026-08-25
Scope: BTC perpetual, 15-minute decisions, 1-hour and 4-hour labels

## Decision

P1.15, the compact sequence baseline, is not authorized as the next model
expansion. Neither horizon produced an eligible candidate that dominates the
no-trade baseline under both base and adverse costs. The final holdout remains
locked and no live or paper-trading authority follows from these development
results.

The 4-hour boosted-stumps result is retained as a research lead, not an edge:
it is positive in all three base-cost folds, but its aggregate adverse-cost
return is negative and its worst Benjamini-Hochberg q-value fails the gate.

## Frozen evaluation setup

- Capture: `data/captures/2026-08-24-btc-15m-580d`
- Three walk-forward folds with the same 365/90/30-day train/validation/test
  calendar, 30-day step, 4-hour-15-minute embargo, and final holdout boundary.
- Prediction horizons: 4 bars (1 hour) and 16 bars (4 hours).
- Identical base/adverse cost scenarios for rules, Ridge, calibrated Logistic,
  and boosted Stumps.
- Moving-block bootstrap: 2,000 repetitions, block length 96 bars.
- Minimum validation/selection trades: 200.
- Final holdout membership is materialized in the manifests but not evaluated.

## Revisit condition

Revisit P1.15 only after a predeclared, bounded change improves adverse-cost
robustness without opening the final holdout—for example a cost-aware abstention
rule or feature/label correction justified independently of these test results.
Repeated hyperparameter search against the same three test folds is not an
acceptable revisit path.
