# P1.29 Gate Decision — Funding Carry v2 (hold 26, exit rule, cost hurdle)

Status: **not justified under the frozen promotion gates**
Date: 2026-09-12
Scope: long Binance spot / short USD-M USDT perpetual pairs, weekly decisions, overlapping
cohorts held 13 or 26 weeks, with a non-positive-funding exit and a cost hurdle

## Decision

Family `funding_carry_panel_v2` is rejected. None of the four pre-registered members is
eligible for further review. The decision report records `no_eligible_member` and the final
holdout remains locked. No live, paper, or shadow authority follows from this.

The family was declared on 2026-09-12 in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-funding-carry-v2-family-design.md`, **after** P1.28's
fold results were read and because of them (section 2 of that spec discloses it). It was
evaluated through the P1.28 pipeline unchanged: the same two captures, the same
walk-forward calendar (split manifest `f33b7b08…`), the same cost tables, statistics and
gates, the same decision module.

All four members clear every statistical gate — bootstrap lower bound above zero, raw
one-sided p-values 0.001 to 0.009, Benjamini-Hochberg q 0.002 to 0.009, 7 of 9 positive
folds, dominance over both controls in base — and all four fail the same three:
the adverse-cost floor, adverse dominance over `no_trade`, and single-fold concentration.
The strongest, `carry_l4w_h26w_exit_hurdle2`, misses the adverse floor by 0.7 bps per
week.

## Frozen evaluation setup

Identical to P1.28 (`P1_28_DECISION_2026-09-11.md`, "Frozen evaluation setup"), plus the
three declared rules: cohorts held 26 weeks (members 1, 3, 4) or 13 (member 2); at every
decision a held pair whose trailing one-week funding is non-positive or absent leaves every
cohort it is in, its share undeployed until the cohort ages out; at cohort formation,
member 4 admits a pair only if `F_4 × 26 / 8 ≥ 2 × c_rt(tier)` with `c_rt` 25 bps (tier
one) or 35 bps (tier two) — 15.4 / 21.5 bps of trailing four-week funding. Controls hold 13
weeks with the four-week lookback (the shortest member's), no exit rule, no hurdle. Each
fold's cohort state is warmed from the 25 Sundays before its first test decision.
220 retained weekly episodes after the same five `UNIVERSE_TOO_SMALL` weeks as P1.28.

The exit rule removed 2.17 pairs per week on average and cut funding *paid* from about 2
to 1.2 bps per week, but a pair whose funding flips mid-week still pays until the next
Sunday. The hurdle rejected 5.2 candidates per week and changed almost nothing: the top
decile by trailing funding already clears twice the round-trip cost. Holding 26 weeks
instead of 13 cut turnover from 0.222 to 0.161 and adverse trading cost from 5.8 to 4.2
bps per week. The exposure the exit rule leaves undeployed (mean gross 0.58) is why the
base total did not rise with the hold.

## What this answers

The three forecast-free levers moved the long-hold carry from −0.092 adverse (P1.28) to
−0.015, from a 9.2 bps to a 0.7 bps per week shortfall, without touching any threshold.
They did not clear the floor, and they cannot: what remains is the assumed slippage (5/10
bps per side, doubled under adverse) and the doubled funding payments, neither of which a
selection rule can reduce further. Whether the carry is real at Binance's actual book is
now a question of measurement, and the Binance cost journal declared the same day
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-binance-cost-journal-design.md`) is the instrument;
its section 5 fixes, before any number exists, how a `funding_carry_panel_v3` may read it.

Two secondary observations:

- With controls holding 13 weeks, `all_pairs_ew` — every positive-funding pair, equal
  weight, no selection — earns 0.065 in base, three quarters of the members. The carry is
  mostly a level effect; selection adds a quarter.
- Concentration in fold 5 is structural to a regime-dependent premium evaluated over one
  spike; it would fall below 0.5 only if the other folds rise, which lower measured costs
  would do uniformly.

## Warm-up and reset effects on the hold comparison

The 26-week members carry two exposure handicaps the 13-week member does not, both
mechanical consequences of declared rules, both visible in the fold reports:

- **Opening warm-up shortfall.** `warm_up_weeks` records intent (25); a warm-up Sunday
  whose universe is below the 40-pair floor forms no cohort. `carry_l4w_h26w` opens fold 0
  at 0.69 gross (Sundays of late 2020 fell below the floor) and fold 4 at 0.88 (its warm-up
  spans the three January 2023 skips); every other fold opens at 0.92 to 1.00. The 13-week
  member opens at 0.85 to 1.00.
- **Mid-fold reset ramp.** A skipped week resets every cohort; a 26-week book then
  rebuilds at 1/26 per week. Fold 3 holds the January 2023 skips at its tenth decision, so
  `carry_l4w_h26w` averages 0.55 gross over that fold against 0.47 for the exit member with
  hold 13 and 0.95 to 1.00 elsewhere. This suppresses fold 3 for the long-hold members and
  therefore inflates their single-fold concentration; it is part of why the largest fold
  share sits at 0.54 to 0.62.

Per-fold mean gross exposure, folds 0 to 8 — `carry_l4w_h26w`: 0.94 0.98 1.00 0.55 0.95
0.92 0.99 0.98 0.97; `carry_l4w_h13w_exit`: 0.62 0.73 0.36 0.47 0.85 0.88 0.87 0.79 0.82;
`carry_l4w_h26w_exit`: 0.39 0.59 0.23 0.31 0.72 0.86 0.74 0.71 0.67. The exit members'
low exposure is the exit rule doing what it was declared to do, not a warm-up effect.

Two further mechanics of the exit rule: it is applied once, at each fold's first decision,
to all 25 warmed cohorts (warm-up Sundays are not decisions), so the opening book is fuller
than a continuously run book would be; and because nothing is carried into a fold's first
episode, that episode's `exit_rule_removals` mean "never entered", not "exited", and are
not turnover. A pair that is both dark and non-paying at a decision is attributed to the
exit rule rather than to the untradeable path; the economics are identical.

## Declared deviations and disclosures

1. **Post-hoc-informed declaration.** The rules were chosen after reading P1.28's fold
   results on the same nine test windows (spec section 2). The family's own out-of-sample
   claim is therefore weaker than P1.28's; the gates were applied unchanged, and the
   holdout was not read.
2. **Exit rule also strips the just-formed cohort**: a pair that clears the four-week
   selection but paid non-positive funding in its last week is never entered.
3. **`extras_mean` for `exit_rule_removals` and `hurdle_rejections`** is a mean over
   retained episodes, like every other extra; `hurdle_rejections` counts only pairs that
   were otherwise rankable (positive trailing funding). Per-episode values are in the fold
   reports.
4. **Identifier drift**: spec section 5 names `hurdle_threshold`; the code and plan use
   `hurdle_minimum_trailing`. No other difference.
5. **P1.28 reproducibility**: the generic control and warm-up rules reduce to P1.28's
   (`carry_l1w_h4w`'s lookback/hold, twelve warm-up Sundays); the v1 fixture's fold-0
   economics are pinned in `tests/fixtures/carry_v1_fold0_expected.json`. Re-running a
   P1.28 fold yields a different `code_hash` than the bound one; the existing P1.28
   artifacts remain the only poolable set for that family.
6. The decision JSON's `declared_deviations` field still carries P1.27's text (known label
   defect, unchanged since P1.28); this record is authoritative.
7. Spot liquidity in 2026 is below the family's 40-pair floor (49 eligible spot legs on
   2026-03-01, 35 on 2026-08-24); the rule was not changed, so the locked holdout would
   skip most weeks under this declaration.

## Revisit condition

No revisit of this family. The next declaration, `funding_carry_panel_v3`, may differ from
this one only by cost tables read from the Binance cost journal's finalisation receipt
under the rule fixed in that journal's spec (section 5): base slippage tiers = median of
instrument medians at 5,000 USDT, adverse = median of instrument p90s at 50,000 USDT,
rounded up to whole basis points, receipt hash cited. If the measured tiers do not clear
the adverse floor, the carry is closed at this venue and scale, and the CTM line stays
`research_only` for want of a base.
