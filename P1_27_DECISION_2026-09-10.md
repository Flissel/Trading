# P1.27 Gate Decision — Cross-Sectional Daily Momentum Panel

Status: **not justified under the frozen promotion gates**
Date: 2026-09-10
Scope: Binance USD-M USDT perpetuals, weekly decisions at the Sunday close, one-week holding

## Decision

Family `xs_momentum_panel_v1` is rejected. None of the six pre-registered members is
eligible for further review. The decision report records `no_eligible_member` and the
final holdout remains locked. No live, paper, or shadow authority follows from this.

The family was declared before any panel data was captured, in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-xs-momentum-family-design.md`, approved 2026-09-09, with
the prospective protocol addendum committed as `PHASE_0_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md` section 16
on the same day and clarified in section 16.1 on 2026-09-10, before any fold was run.

The strongest member, `ts_mom_4w`, is retained as a research lead and not as an edge. It is
base-positive, adverse-non-negative, positive in enough folds, and dominates both controls.
It fails because its return cannot be distinguished from zero: the 95% block-bootstrap lower
bound is below zero, its raw one-sided p-value of 0.1814 would not pass even uncorrected, and
60% of its pooled net PnL comes from a single fold.

## Frozen evaluation setup

- Capture: `data/captures/2026-09-10-binance-um-usdt-perps-1d-repaired`, 860 USDT
  perpetuals including delisted contracts, 637,308 daily bars, 2,629,011 funding rows.
  Derived from `2026-09-10-binance-um-usdt-perps-1d` by filling 318 of 323 interior gaps
  from Binance's daily kline dumps.
- Nine walk-forward folds, each 365 days train, 91 validation, 182 test, 182-day step,
  14-day embargo applied twice per fold. Test windows tile 2021-05-03 to 2025-10-27.
- 225 pooled out-of-sample weekly episodes against a floor of 200. One episode is one
  weekly portfolio rebalance; the contracts inside it are not independent samples.
- Final holdout of 26 rebalances from 2026-03-08, materialised in the manifest and not
  evaluated.
- Moving-block bootstrap: 2,000 repetitions, block length 4 weeks, seed 17, confidence 0.95.
- Costs: fee 5 bps per side both scenarios; slippage 5 bps tier one and 10 tier two in base,
  doubled in adverse; funding from actual historical rates, with receipts zeroed and payments
  doubled in adverse; turnover-based charging; forced closes at the last available close.
- No parameter was fitted. All six members are fixed by declaration, so the training and
  validation windows serve geometry and history only.

## Why each member failed

- **xs_mom_1w, xs_mom_12w, ts_mom_12w, xs_rev_1w** are base-negative outright and fail
  every economic gate including dominance over `no_trade`.
- **xs_mom_4w** is base-positive by 0.041 over 225 weeks, which is 1.8 basis points per week
  and below its own bootstrap lower bound. It is adverse-negative and grossly concentrated:
  one fold, one contract and one episode each account for more than the entire pooled total.
- **ts_mom_4w** is the only member that clears the base mean, the adverse floor, the fold
  fraction and both dominance comparisons. It fails on three counts. The 95% block-bootstrap
  lower bound is -0.0046, so zero is inside the interval. Its raw one-sided p-value is 0.1814,
  which fails a 0.10 gate before any correction and gives a Benjamini-Hochberg q of 0.975
  across the family of six. And 60.1% of its pooled base net PnL comes from a single fold,
  above the 50% concentration limit. Its maximum drawdown is 53.2% and its win rate 51.6%.

## What this answers

The question this family was built to settle was whether a simple, few-parameter momentum
signal survives realistic costs and multiple-testing correction on **some** timescale, after
P1.15 rejected every candidate at the 15-minute cadence with 1-hour and 4-hour labels.

The answer is no, on a weekly cadence over a 860-contract panel spanning four and a half
out-of-sample years. That closes the "wrong timescale" objection to P1.15 rather than leaving
it open.

Two secondary observations, neither of which is a gate result:

- Time-series momentum outperformed cross-sectional momentum, as the 2025 comparative
  literature reports. `ts_mom_4w` is the only member with a positive annualised Sharpe worth
  the name, and both cross-sectional momentum members with a meaningful lookback are negative.
- Costs remain the binding constraint, as in P1.15. Every member's adverse-cost total is far
  below its base-cost total, and only `ts_mom_4w` stays non-negative under adverse costs.

## Declared deviations

Both are recorded in the decision report itself and repeated here.

1. The cross-sectional quintile size and the time-series weight cap are computed from the
   contracts actually rankable at a decision, not from the full eligible universe as spec
   section 8.1 states, because a contract can be eligible yet lack the exact lagged close or a
   complete volatility window.
2. Spec section 7.1 authorises excluding a week from the pooled series only at the universe
   level, where no episode and no cost exist. A week in which a single member holds nothing is
   excluded from that member's own pooled series the same way. The unwind cost such a week can
   carry is never discarded: it is rolled into that member's next retained episode in the same
   fold, or backward onto the fold's last retained episode when none follows. No member
   recorded such a week in this run.

## Data limitation

ICPUSDT lacks five consecutive days, 2022-09-22 to 2022-09-26, absent from both Binance's
monthly and daily dumps and confirmed 404 at capture time. Those days are named in the
walk-forward manifest under `absent_at_source_days`. The contract is dormant across that
period rather than delisted: its close is flat at 6.44 with zero quote volume for 103 of the
104 preceding days, and it resumes on 2022-09-27. It is outside the eligible universe at all
five decisions whose windows straddle the gap, so the limitation changed no result.

## Revisit condition

Revisit this family only after a predeclared, bounded change that does not depend on these
test results. Re-running the same members against the same folds with different universe
thresholds, lookbacks or cost assumptions is not an acceptable revisit path, and the final
holdout remains closed.

The honest next questions, in order of prior:

1. Mechanical funding and basis carry, which requires no forecast and whose binding
   constraint is capital and execution rather than signal. The OKX public cost journal that
   is accumulating serves this.
2. A CTREND-style aggregate over many technical signals, which the 2025 evidence reports as
   materially stronger than plain momentum and which renders plain momentum insignificant in
   its presence. That is a new family with a new declaration, not a revisit of this one.

The CTM mixture-of-experts line stays `research_only`. Its premise was abstention and regime
state over a base with an edge, and no such base exists.
