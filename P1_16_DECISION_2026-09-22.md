# P1.16 Gate Decision — CTM Feasibility Spike (C.0 real-data checkpoint)

Status: **retired on this dataset; CTM stays research_only**
Date: 2026-09-22 (user decision, option 1 of three presented)
Scope: BTC perpetual, 15-minute bars, 580-day capture, 4-bar horizon, walk-forward fold 0,
seed 0, family `ctm_regime_gated_mixture` (`configs/neural-mixture-v1.json` on branch
`codex/phase-a-edge-recovery`)

## Decision

The CTM feasibility line (P1.16) and its residual/uncertainty challenger (P1.17) are
**retired on the 580-day BTC 15-minute capture**. No candidate is authorized, no gate is
passed, and Plan 2 of the C.0 design (vendored CTM reference, `ctm_alone`/`ctm_moe`, the
section-7 decision artifact) is **not** started. The final holdout remains locked; no live,
paper, or shadow authority follows.

The CTM *idea* is not rejected: its premise was always abstention or regime gating **over a
base that already has an edge** (PHASE_0_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL section 10). That base does
not exist on 15-minute BTC price direction — P1.15 and this checkpoint agree — and the only
base in this repository that has cleared every frozen gate so far is the small-book funding
carry (P1.33, provisional as of 2026-09-17; official decision pending the Binance cost
journal). If P1.33's official decision confirms, the admissible next use of the CTM is a
pre-registered abstention/regime layer over that carry, declared before any of its results
are seen. Nothing about that future family is decided here.

for any cell, because those are written only on a completed evaluation.

## Why (root cause, verified against the code)

Every cell shares one decision rule, `trading_bot.abstention.decide_with_abstention`: a bar
trades only when the entire lower bound of the forecast interval clears round-trip cost on
the long side, or the entire upper bound clears it on the short side. On this capture, at
this horizon, forecast intervals rarely clear cost, so nearly every bar abstains before any
threshold or floor is applied. The `none/none` cell is provably the frozen base logistic on
its own (the mixture's fixed weights are all zero), so its outcome restates P1.15 under this
campaign's floor; the fixed-weight mixtures fail identically; the `gru/learned` cell trained
an encoder specifically meant to improve calibration and interval width, converged within
its budget, and still could not clear the floor.

The failure is the designed, registered path: `run_neural_fold` records
`status: "failed"` with the error code before re-raising, exactly as the sibling Phase-A
runner's tests pin for the same code. No source code changed; no threshold was touched.

## Protocol consequences

- Section 10 (CTM-specific gate): no forecast-skill, ensemble, calibration, or regime
  claim can be made, because no cell produced a comparable completed evaluation. The
  "must beat a GRU-encoder twin" requirement was never reached.
- Section 15 (revisit conditions): "observed trade frequency makes the effective-sample
  threshold impractical" is now triggered by evidence on this dataset. Per the same
  section, any relaxation is prospective only; the 200-trade floor is not weakened for
  these four already-evaluated attempts, and they are not re-run under a looser floor.
- Options not taken, by the user's choice: a new geometry on the same data (the h16 split
  exists; a longer horizon widens the intervals and would need its own pre-registration),
  and a prospective change to the floor for future families.

## What this closes and what stays open

Closed: P1.16 and P1.17 on the 580-day BTC 15-minute capture; the C.0 review gate as
scoped; Plan 2. The `codex/phase-a-edge-recovery` branch (Phase-A pipeline, neural runner,
campaign registry, this checkpoint's docs) is complete as research tooling and stays
unmerged; whether to merge it into `codex/phase1-foundation` is a separate integration
decision that has been pending with the user since C.0's suite passed.

Open, conditional on P1.33's official decision: a pre-registered CTM abstention/regime
family over the small-book funding carry. It would be a new family with its own
declaration, its own fold reports, and its own record; it inherits nothing from this
attempt except the lesson that the base must carry the edge.
