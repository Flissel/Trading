# P1.31 Gate Decision — Trend Aggregate Panel (directional)

Status: **not justified under the frozen promotion gates**
Date: 2026-09-15
Scope: Binance USD-M USDT perpetuals, weekly decisions at the Sunday close, a twelve-indicator
trend vote as the signal, one-week and four-week holds, time-series and cross-sectional books

## Decision

Family `trend_aggregate_panel_v1` is rejected. None of the four pre-registered members is
eligible for further review. The decision report records `no_eligible_member` and the final
holdout remains locked. No live, paper, or shadow authority follows from this.

The family was declared on 2026-09-15 in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-15-trend-aggregate-family-design.md` before any fold ran,
and evaluated through the P1.27 pipeline unchanged: the same capture, calendar, universe
rule, cost tables, statistics, gates and decision module. Its three controls reproduce
P1.27's pooled totals to the last digit (`random_ranks` −0.50973 / −1.67331,
`passive_long_ew` −0.35137 / −1.23996), which is the strongest available check that the
shared path did not move.

The three time-series members are base-negative and adverse-negative in aggregate, with
negative annualised Sharpe. The cross-sectional member `ta_xs_q5` is base-positive at
+20.6 bps per week over 225 weeks, positive in 7 of 9 folds, with a raw one-sided p-value
of 0.072 — but its bootstrap lower bound sits below zero, its Benjamini-Hochberg q across
four members is 0.29, and under adverse costs it loses 31 bps per week. It is a lead, not
an edge.

## Frozen evaluation setup

- Capture `data/captures/2026-09-10-binance-um-usdt-perps-1d-repaired` (P1.27's; 860 USDT
  perpetuals, 637,308 daily bars, 2,629,011 funding settlements); nine walk-forward folds
  with the P1.27 geometry tiling 2021-05-09 to 2025-10-19, 225 pooled weekly episodes, no
  skipped week, holdout of 26 rebalances from 2026-03-08 locked.
- Signal: mean of twelve votes (SMA 20/50/100 vs price, SMA crossovers 20/50 and 50/100,
  breakouts 20/50/100, rate of change 20/60/120, MACD 12/26), each a calendar-complete
  span; a contract is scored only when its last 121 calendar days are complete. On average
  96.1 of 99.7 eligible contracts per week were scored (96.4%; minimum 80).
- Members: `ta_ts_t02` (long score ≥ 0.2, short ≤ −0.2, one week), `ta_ts_t05` (threshold
  0.5), `ta_ts_t02_h4w` (threshold 0.2, four overlapping weekly cohorts of one quarter of
  capital), `ta_xs_q5` (long top quintile, short bottom quintile by score). Time-series
  weights are P1.27's inverse-volatility construction with a 2/n water-filled cap; the
  cross-sectional book is P1.27's quintile construction (minimum quintile size 8).
- Costs: fee 5 bps per side, slippage 5/10 bps by tier (doubled under adverse), funding
  from actual settlements with receipts zeroed and payments doubled under adverse, forced
  closes ×1 / ×2. Bootstrap 2,000 × block 4 × seed 17, BH q ≤ 0.10 over four members.

Two things stand out. The time-series vote is a market-wide direction bet — `mean_score`
swings between +0.7 and −0.7 across weeks, so at threshold 0.2 the book is long or short
almost everything at once — and its gross return is negative before any cost: the trend
vote did not predict the next week's sign. The cross-sectional book is different in kind:
+25.8 bps gross per week is a genuine ranking effect that survives base costs, and 7.9 bps
of it are funding received on the short leg (downtrending names pay their shorts). Under
the declared adverse rule that receipt vanishes and payments double, and the 0.94 weekly
turnover pays doubled slippage; both together are −52 bps against +25.8 gross.

## What this answers

Price-only directional work on this panel is closed at this cadence. Plain momentum
(P1.27) could not be told from zero; the twelve-indicator aggregate that the literature
reports as stronger is, as a time-series signal, strictly worse — negative gross — on a
weekly rebalance over 2021–2025. The cross-sectional ranking is the only directional
construction in this repository with a positive gross return after base costs, and it
fails the same two things every candidate has failed: significance after correction and
the adverse-cost floor.

The spec's own decision rule (section 7) applies: the next directional declaration must
bring a new information source, not another transformation of the same closes. The
exception this record names is narrow: `ta_xs_q5`'s adverse loss is dominated by the
funding asymmetry, so a cross-sectional trend book on *measured* costs and with the funding
leg accounted the way P1.28 accounts it (receipts ×0.75, not ×0) is a legitimate follow-up
question — as a new declaration, after P1.30.

## Declared deviations and disclosures

1. **Design informed by P1.27's record** (spec section 2): the family was chosen from the
   literature after P1.27's rejection; the four-week member and the time-series weight
   construction follow P1.27's findings. The holdout was never read.
2. **Warm-up.** Each fold forms weight vectors on the three Sundays before its first test
   decision (no episode, nothing carried), so the four-week member opens on a full book;
   reason code `FOLD_OPENING_BOOK_WARMED_FROM_PRIOR_WEEKS`, `warm_up_weeks: 3` in every fold
   report. Those Sundays lie in the embargo/validation span and read only data at or before
   themselves. The pooled decision artifact does not carry fold reason codes; this record
   does.
3. **Scored versus eligible.** The universe admits a contract on 91 daily bars; a score
   needs 121 complete calendar days. 96.4% of eligible contract-weeks were scored; the
   unscored ones held nothing in any member but counted in `passive_long_ew`. The decision
   JSON's `declared_deviations` text describes P1.27's rankable-subset reasons, not this one;
   this record is authoritative.
4. **Out-of-universe cohort holdings** of the four-week member are charged tier-two
   slippage on exit (`panel_accounting`'s default) — a path P1.27 never exercised.
5. **Thresholds are net-vote gates**: 0.2 means at least 3 of 12 net votes, 0.5 at least 6.
   `abstained_contracts` counts scored contracts inside the dead band only. The MACD's EMAs
   run over the contract's whole observed series and are therefore the one indicator whose
   value depends on the capture's start (2020-01-01); the retained weight of the oldest
   observations is below 1e-4.
6. **Post-run precision fix (commit 9a2a196).** The first decision attempt refused to pool:
   the four-week book leaves residual weights of about 1e-28 where a long in an older vector
   nearly cancels a short in a newer one, and those residuals' contributions need about 60
   digits to sum exactly, beyond the 50-digit exact context. The pooling precision was raised
   to 120 digits; exact sums are unchanged at any sufficient precision, and P1.29's decision
   re-derived to its identical report hash before the trend decision was re-run. The nine
   fold reports were not touched (the decision module is not in the fold code hash).
7. `roc_n` is computed as sign(P_t − P_{t−n}) (identical for positive prices, exact at
   zero); windows are calendar spans; both clarified in the spec before any fold ran.
8. Re-running any P1.27, P1.28 or P1.29 fold now yields a different `code_hash` (shared
   modules changed); their bound artifacts remain the only poolable sets for those families.

## Revisit condition

No revisit of this family. Price-only directional families are closed on this panel at
this cadence. The admissible next directional declaration is one of: a cross-sectional
trend book on measured costs with P1.28's funding treatment (after P1.30), or a family that
brings a new information source (order-book state from the cost journal, funding and basis
regime, on-chain flows). The CTM mixture-of-experts line stays `research_only`: there is
still no base with an edge under the declared adverse costs.
