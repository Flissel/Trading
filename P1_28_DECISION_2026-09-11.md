# P1.28 Gate Decision — Funding Carry Panel

Status: **not justified under the frozen promotion gates**
Date: 2026-09-11
Scope: long Binance spot / short USD-M USDT perpetual pairs, weekly decisions at the
Sunday close, overlapping cohorts held 4 or 13 weeks

## Decision

Family `funding_carry_panel_v1` is rejected. None of the three pre-registered members is
eligible for further review. The decision report records `no_eligible_member` and the final
holdout remains locked. No live, paper, or shadow authority follows from this.

The family was declared before any spot data was captured, in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-funding-carry-family-design.md`, approved 2026-09-11, and
evaluated through the P1.27 walk-forward manifest, block bootstrap and decision gates unchanged.

The strongest member, `carry_l4w_h13w`, is retained as a research lead. It is the first
candidate in this repository to clear every statistical gate: its base mean is positive, its
95% block-bootstrap lower bound is above zero, its raw one-sided p-value is 0.005 and its
Benjamini-Hochberg q across the three members is 0.015, and eight of nine folds are positive.
It fails on cost and on concentration: under the adverse cost assumption its pooled net return
is negative and below `no_trade`, and 53% of its pooled base net PnL comes from a single fold.

## Why each member failed

- **carry_l1w_h4w** and **carry_l4w_h4w** fail the bootstrap lower bound, the adverse
  floor, the fold fraction, the multiple-testing gate, the concentration limit and adverse
  dominance. Their turnover is roughly twice the long-hold member's, and the extra week of
  funding a short lookback buys does not pay for it.
- **carry_l4w_h13w** clears the base mean, the bootstrap lower bound, the raw p-value, the
  Benjamini-Hochberg q, the fold fraction, base dominance over both controls and both
  concentration limits on pairs and episodes. It fails three gates: its adverse total is
  -0.0923, below zero and below `no_trade`, and one fold carries 53.2% of its pooled PnL.

## What this answers

The question was whether mechanical funding carry, which needs no forecast, survives
realistic two-leg execution on a weekly cadence. Under base costs it does, and for the first
time in this repository a candidate's return is distinguishable from zero after correction.
Under the declared adverse assumption — doubled slippage and a quarter of the receipts
withheld — it does not, and the pooled result leans on the 2024 funding spike.

Three observations, none a gate result:

- Holding longer is strictly better here: 13 weeks beats 4 weeks on every metric, because
  the cost of a two-leg round trip is amortised over more settlements while the funding a
  pair pays decays slowly. The lookback matters less than the hold.
- `all_pairs_ew`, the equal-weighted book of every positive-funding pair, earns 0.0496 in
  base, about half the long-hold member. Selecting the top decile adds something, but the
  bulk of the carry is a level effect across the whole panel.
- Costs are the binding constraint for the third time in a row (P1.15, P1.27, P1.28). The
  adverse scenario was declared as the fail-closed one, and it is the one that failed.

## Declared deviations and post-freeze changes

All were decided before the corresponding data was read, and none changes a declared
parameter or a gate.

1. **Opening book warm-up.** Each fold's cohort state is warmed from the twelve Sundays before
   its first test decision — universe and cohort selection only, formed on data at or before
   each Sunday, no episode evaluated, carried weights empty so the first test episode pays its
   full entry turnover inside the window. Without it the 13-week member would have spent half
   of every 26-week fold ramping up, an 18% haircut on its primary metric that the 4-week
   members do not bear. Reason code `FOLD_OPENING_BOOK_WARMED_FROM_PRIOR_WEEKS`.
2. **Forced closes keep capital undeployed.** A pair whose leg loses its exit bar is force-closed
   at one side and removed from every retained cohort; its cohort share stays undeployed
   until the cohort ages out rather than being reinvested in its siblings.
3. **Controls** use the 1-week lookback and 4-week hold of `carry_l1w_h4w`; the spec names a
   size and hold for `random_pairs` but no lookback. `random_pairs` therefore turns over about
   twice as much as the long-hold member, which makes the dominance floor easiest for
   `carry_l4w_h13w`; the gate is a floor and did not decide anything here.
4. **Pair count 470, not 467.** The spike classified every `1000` prefix as scaled; three
   perpetuals (`1000CATUSDT`, `1000CHEEMSUSDT`, `1000SATSUSDT`) are listed on spot under the
   same symbol and are direct pairs. Spec section 3.2 was corrected before the capture.
5. **Spec 7.1 rule 4** (at least one settlement in the lookback) is applied at cohort
   selection, not at eligibility; every pair clearing the liquidity floor settles every eight
   hours, so the universe count is unaffected.
6. **Timestamp parser fix (commit 0deccdd).** Binance's spot daily dumps switched to 16-digit
   microsecond timestamps with the 2025-01 files; parsed as milliseconds they broke the
   dataset build after the download. The parser now detects the unit and floors microsecond
   stamps to millisecond precision so a spot close matches the perpetual calendar exactly.
7. **Warm-up delisting fix (commit 1aeedc4).** A pair delisted between a warm-up Sunday and
   the fold's first decision (`MATICUSDT` at the POL migration, fold 7) sat in the warmed
   cohorts with no entry bar. The runner now drops any pair whose leg has no bar at the
   decision before assembling the book. Folds 0–6 were re-run so all nine share one code hash.
8. **Dominance controls are selected by declared kind** (`no_trade`, `random_pairs`), not by
   position or literal name; P1.27's decision is unchanged by construction.
9. The decision JSON's `declared_deviations` field carries P1.27's two texts, because the
   decision module reuses that constant for every family; they do not describe this family.
   This record is the authoritative list. Fixing the label would change the decision module
   and is deferred to the next family.
10. Re-running any P1.27 fold now yields a different `code_hash` (`evaluate_episode` gained an
    optional parameter), and a fresh capture of the P1.27 symbols a different
    `capture_root_hash` (the manifest gained `market`/`venue`); the existing P1.27 artifacts
    remain the only poolable set for that family and still verify.

## Contamination disclosure

Two spikes on 2026-09-11 read the perpetual capture's funding aggregates through 2026-08,
which includes the locked holdout from 2026-03-08, before the family was declared. They
informed the choice of lookbacks and holds. No spot data and no per-fold result was seen
before the declaration; the holdout itself was never evaluated by any fold.

## Data limitations

- Twelve spot instruments have interior gaps proven absent at source and named in the
  manifest: FTTUSDT 310 days, USDCUSDT 165, CVCUSDT 153, KEYUSDT 27, LUNAUSDT 17, VIDTUSDT 8,
  STRAXUSDT 7, BNXUSDT 5, and three days each for BTCSTUSDT, COCOSUSDT, QUICKUSDT, SUNUSDT.
  These are trading suspensions; the calendar-complete liquidity window excludes the affected
  pairs across each gap. ICPUSDT's five absent perpetual days carry over from P1.27.
- Spot liquidity thinned the universe below 40 pairs in five weeks of 2023; those weeks were
  skipped at universe level with exit costs uncharged, as the protocol authorises.

## Revisit condition

Revisit this family only after a predeclared, bounded change that does not depend on these
test results. Re-running the same members with softer adverse assumptions is not an
acceptable path: the adverse scenario was declared as the fail-closed one and it failed.

The honest next questions, in order of prior:

1. **Execution, not signal.** `carry_l4w_h13w` needs about 1.3 bps per week per unit of
   capital of cost reduction to survive the adverse scenario. The OKX public cost journal that
   is accumulating (10,000 observations from 2026-09-15) exists to replace the assumed
   slippage tiers with measured ones; a family re-declared on measured costs is a new family.
2. **Longer holds** than 13 weeks and a maker-side spot leg are the two levers this result
   points at, both to be declared before any data is read.
3. A CTREND-style aggregate remains the next signal question, as the P1.27 record said.

The CTM mixture-of-experts line stays `research_only`: there is still no base with an edge
under the declared adverse costs.
