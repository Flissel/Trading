# P1.33 Gate Decision — Funding Carry v4 (declared capital, slot book, measured costs)

Status: **eligible member available — first candidate under the frozen promotion gates**
Date: 2026-09-22
Scope: long Binance spot / short USD-M USDT perpetual pairs on a declared book of at most
10,000 USDT held in ten equal pair slots (at most 500 USDT per leg per order, standard
taker fees), weekly decisions at the Sunday close, slots held up to 13 or 26 weeks; slippage
tiers read from the Binance cost journal's finalisation receipt under the capital-declared
rule fixed in the journal's spec (section 5.1) before the receipt existed

## Decision

Family `funding_carry_panel_v4_measured` has two eligible members. The decision report
records `eligible_member_available` for `carry_s10_l4w_h13w_exit` and
`carry_s10_l4w_h26w_exit`; the two members without the exit rule are rejected on the
adverse floor. This is the first time in this repository that any pre-registered member has
cleared every frozen gate — statistical, adverse-cost, concentration and dominance.

The record applies one further reading the family's spec fixed in advance (section 3.3):
because a slot book has no warmed cohorts, its uncharged final exit is larger than a cohort
book's, and the record subtracts it from any member that passes narrowly. After that
subtraction `carry_s10_l4w_h26w_exit` stays positive under adverse costs (+0.01261) and
`carry_s10_l4w_h13w_exit` does not (−0.00502). **The release candidate is
`carry_s10_l4w_h26w_exit`**, which is also the member protocol section 16.2 derives for the
holdout read (highest adverse total after that subtraction). The final holdout stays
locked; reading it is a separate, explicit user decision. No live, paper, or shadow
authority follows from this record.

Three observations.

1. **The small book earns more than the large one because it stays invested.** On the same
   capture, folds and receipt, P1.30's `carry_l4w_h26w_exit` collects 6.6 bps of funding
   per week at a mean gross exposure of 0.58 — the cohort exit rule leaves the freed share
   idle until the cohort ages out. The slot book refills a vacated slot from the ranking the
   same week (gross 0.98) and collects 10.0 bps. The exit rule is what makes the difference
   under adverse costs in both families; here it also keeps the capital working.
2. **Execution at 500 USDT is cheap and the adverse charge is nearly the base charge.** The
   worse leg costs 1 / 2 bps per side base and 1 / 5 adverse, against P1.30's 1 / 4 and
   3 / 16 at ten times the order size. Adverse trading cost is 3.1 bps per week against
   4.5 bps of discounted funding; the margin is 1.0 bps per week, 0.6 after the uncharged
   final exit.
3. **The price leg is neutral.** −0.1 to −0.4 bps per week of gross price return in every
   member: the hedge holds, and nothing in the result is a directional bet.

## What this answers

The carry line's question since P1.28 was whether a spot-hedged funding carry pays its own
execution under the adverse rule. P1.28 and P1.29 said not at assumed costs; P1.30 says not
at measured costs for a book of several million USDT. P1.33 says yes for the book the user
would actually run — under 10,000 USDT, ten slots, 500 USDT per leg, standard taker fees —
with the exit rule and a 26-week hold. The premium is the same funding premium every
declaration found; what changed is that the cost model matches the capital, and that the
slot book does not leave capital idle.

This is a development-set result on nine locked folds. Protocol section 2.3 allows one
holdout read for a named release candidate; section 16.2 fixes how it is done. The
candidate the holdout machinery (P1.34) would derive from this decision is
`carry_s10_l4w_h26w_exit`. Reading it requires captures extended through the September
2026 dumps (the last holdout episode exits 2026-09-06; Binance publishes September's funding
dump in early October) and the user's explicit go; a dry run (`carry-holdout --check-only`)
confirms readiness without spending the read.

## Declared deviations and disclosures

1. **Design informed by earlier records** (spec section 2): the book, the exit rule and the
   26-week hold recombine what P1.28, P1.29 and P1.32 showed on the same nine test windows;
   the capital block came from two facts the user supplied on 2026-09-15 when asked (book
   under 10,000 USDT; standard taker without BNB). Every rule was fixed in the spec on
   2026-09-16 before a fold ran; the holdout was never read.
2. **The official numbers were seen in advance.** On 2026-09-17 a provisional chain was run
   on an assumed-cost declaration carrying the interim journal preview's declared-table
   equivalents (base 1/2, adverse 1/5 bps, from a read-only preview at 3,580 rounds taken
   2026-09-15). The receipt at 10,003 segments produced the same four declared values, so
   the official fold reports and decision equal the provisional ones to the digit. The
   preview and the provisional run are cost data seen early, not a rule chosen from them:
   the section-5.1 reading was fixed on 2026-09-16 and copies section 5's structure.
3. **Receipt at the eligibility floor; non-uniform journal sample; first finalisation
   refused.** As disclosed for P1.30 (its items 1 to 3): receipt at 10,003 segments against
   an 11,000-round target after the chain trigger was lowered to the 10,000 floor on
   2026-09-17; 10,003 rounds taken of 14,342 possible over 10.13 days (machine sleep, the
   2026-09-17 aborted shutdown, daily restarts); the P1.30 chain's first `finalize` refused
   on a chain-head read during an append, and this family's chain produced the receipt 59
   seconds later. `verify: ok` at 10,196 segments.
4. **`all_pairs_ew` is not P1.28's control.** It runs unwarmed under this family (spec 4.3,
   corrected 2026-09-16 before any fold), so its book ramps 1/13 per week at every fold
   start; its base total 0.09086 is not comparable to P1.30's 0.08713. It is context, never
   a gate.
5. **Extras.** `slot_releases` counts age-outs and lost-bar releases together;
   `slot_fills` counts every fill including a same-decision re-fill of a released pair
   (whose weights do not change and whose hold clock restarts); `exit_rule_removals` is per
   episode across all slots; a pair both dark and non-paying at a decision is counted as a
   release, not an exit. All are means over retained episodes in `extras_mean`.
6. **Uncharged final exit is reported per fold and scenario and subtracted in this record**,
   not in the decision module (spec 3.3). The subtraction changes no gate; it is the reason
   this record names one release candidate rather than two.
7. **Delisted contracts** (P1.32, item 8): 124 of the 860 perpetuals have dead klines past
   their last funding month; the universe's liquidity floor removes them within weeks and a
   held one is force-closed. `base_forced_close_count` is in the decision artifact per
   member; no rule was changed.
8. **Code hash and the cohort path.** `code_hash` `89b2fb19…` is shared with P1.30's
   reports; the slot mode was added beside the cohort mode with the v1 fold-0 economics
   pinned and the v2 tests unchanged, and the runner's decision loop was later extracted for
   the holdout read with its output unchanged.
9. The decision JSON's `declared_deviations` block carries P1.27's texts (known label
   defect); this record is authoritative.
10. **2026 spot liquidity** (P1.29, item 7): the eligible spot universe in 2026 is at or
    below the family's 40-pair floor on several Sundays, so the locked holdout would skip
    some of its 26 decisions under this declaration; the holdout read refuses if more than
    four are skipped (protocol 16.2).
